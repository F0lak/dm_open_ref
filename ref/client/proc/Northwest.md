
## Northwest (proc)

**Format:**
+   Northwest()

**Returns:**
+   Returns 1 on success; 0 on failure.

**Called When:**
+   Called when the player presses the "up-left" key or cursor.

**Default Action:**
+   Calls src.Move() towards the northwest.
