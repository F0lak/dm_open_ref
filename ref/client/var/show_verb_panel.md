
## show_verb_panel (var)

**Default Value:**
+   1
***
Setting this to 0 turns off the verb panel in Dream Seeker. You might want to do that, for instance, if you've only got one verb (like "say") and the panel looks stupid with just one verb in it.


```dm

client
   show_verb_panel = 0

```
