
## floor (proc)

**Format:**
+   floor(A)

**Arguments:**
+   A: A number, pixloc, or vector.

**Returns:**
+   the floor of A
***
Returns the floor of A (the largest integer less than or equal to A).


```dm

usr << floor(1.45) // outputs 1

usr << floor(-1.45) // outputs -2

```
