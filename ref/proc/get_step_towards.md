
## get_step_towards (proc)

**Format:**
+   get_step_towards(Ref, Trg)

**Arguments:**
+   Ref: Starting point or object.
+   Trg: An object on the map.

**Returns:**
+   The location of the new position.
***
Calculate the position of a step from `Ref` in the direction of `Trg`.
