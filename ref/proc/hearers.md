
## hearers (proc)

**Format:**
+   hearers(Depth=world.view,Center=usr)
***
This is just like <code>viewers()</code>, but it is a list of mobs that can hear the center object. Currently, this is computed on the assumption that opaque objects block sound, just like they block light.
