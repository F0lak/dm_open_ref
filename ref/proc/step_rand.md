
## step_rand (proc)

**Format:**
+   step_rand(Ref,Speed=0)

**Arguments:**
+   Ref: A mob or obj.
+   Speed: Speed to move, in pixels. 0 uses Ref.step_size.

**Returns:**
+   1 on success; 0 otherwise.
***
Move Ref randomly.
