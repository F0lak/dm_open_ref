
## vector (proc)

**Format:**
+   vector(x,y)
+   vector(x,y,z)
+   vector(String)
+   vector(List)
+   vector(Vector)
+   vector(Pixloc)

**Arguments:**
+   x,y,z: Components of the new vector.
+   String: A text string to parse as a vector, where the delimiter between numbers can be a comma or x.
+   List: A numeric list to copy as a vector.
+   Vector: A vector to copy.
+   Vector: A pixloc to copy as a vector.

**Returns:**
+   A new vector.
***
Creates a vector.
